# 🎃 Interactive Fortune Wheel — Super Sexy Halloween Edition 🩸

A high-performance, full-featured, and customizable **Interactive Fortune Wheel** web application with rich multi-sentence descriptions, smooth realistic physics, procedural audio synthesis, and full sector management.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Super Sexy Halloween Theme & Classic Luxury Mode 🎃
- **Atmospheric Visuals**: Deep midnight velvet background with mystical fog, floating bats (`🦇`), glowing embers, and pulsing Jack-o'-lantern lights.
- **Wrought-Iron & Blood-Gold Rim**: Gothic 3D rim with glowing ruby & pumpkin neon studs that pulse during spin.
- **Stiletto Dagger Pointer**: Seductive stiletto flapper with gold filigree and a bleeding ruby jewel cap.
- **Center Hub**: Seductive glowing core with interactive **"🎃 SPIN"** button.
- **Theme Switcher**: Instant toggle between `🎃 Halloween` and `✨ Classic` in the top header.

### 2. Effortless Sector Management & Editing
- **Direct Click on Wheel**: Click on **any sector directly on the wheel** to immediately open and edit its title, full description, and color!
- **Edit Sectors Drawer**:
  - Add any number of sectors with rich titles and multi-sentence descriptions.
  - Pick from curated color swatches or any custom hex color.
  - Reorder sectors up (`▲`) and down (`▼`).
  - Delete unwanted sectors with safety confirmation.
  - Click any sector card in the list or the **[Edit]** badge to edit it directly.
  - Automatic smooth scroll to the form with active pulse highlight.
- **JSON Import / Export**: Save and load custom sector presets with one click.
- **Reset to Defaults**: Quick restore to default templates.

### 3. Rich Presets & Scenarios
- 🎃 **Sexy Halloween: Sins & Desires**: Seductive party challenges (Vampire's Kiss, Potion of Seduction, Deal with the Devil, Witch's Dance, Web of Passion, Wicked Secret, Midnight Rendezvous, Cursed Gold).
- 🎁 **Premium Prizes & Rewards**: High-end prizes, gadgets, VIP passes, and mystery boxes.
- 🎯 **Decision Maker**: Fun and practical decision resolver (Go For It, Sleep on It, Plan B, etc.).
- 🌴 **Weekend & Night Plans**: Movie marathons, gourmet cooking, game nights, and concerts.
- 💼 **Team Building & Retro**: Warm retro check-ins and fun team building prompts.

### 4. Mathematical Accuracy & Physics
- **100% Mathematical Precision**: Pointer angle at 12 o'clock and final rotation angle are strictly synchronized via trigonometric mapping.
- **Procedural Web Audio API**: Offline procedural ticking sounds with dynamic speed pitch shifts and victorious gothic fanfare.
- **Spooky Confetti Burst**: Center explosion with flutter bats, mini pumpkins, and glowing embers.
- **Zoom & Fullscreen**: Scale the wheel from 60% to 140% or trigger Fullscreen (`F`).
- **Zen Mode**: Hide the header for clean presentation mode.
- **Spin History**: Automatic log with timestamp, winner name, and stats.

---

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

- **`Spacebar`** — Spin the wheel
- **`F`** — Toggle fullscreen mode
- **`+` / `-`** — Zoom in / Zoom out wheel
- **`Esc`** — Close drawer or winner modal

---

## 🚀 Running the Project

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser or run:
```bash
open http://localhost:8888
```
Or start a fresh local server:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8888
```
